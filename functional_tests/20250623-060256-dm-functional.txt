**Functional Test Cases**

- **Test Case 1: Credit Card Due Reminder Notification**
  - **Input:** Cardholder with an upcoming payment due date.
  - **Expected Output:** Automated notification is sent to the cardholder reminding them of their upcoming credit card payment due date.
  - **Edge Case:** Cardholder changes their contact details; ensure the reminder is sent to the updated contact information.

- **Test Case 2: Overdue Balance Alert**
  - **Input:** Cardholder misses their payment due date.
  - **Expected Output:** Alert is sent notifying the cardholder of the overdue balance and potential consequences.
  - **Edge Case:** Cardholder has multiple credit cards; ensure alert specifies which card's payment is overdue.

- **Test Case 3: Collection Notification**
  - **Input:** Account becomes significantly delinquent.
  - **Expected Output:** Formal collection notification is sent detailing the amount owed and any additional charges.
  - **Edge Case:** Verify notification is not sent if the cardholder has already initiated a payment plan.

- **Test Case 4: Payment Plan Proposal**
  - **Input:** Cardholder unable to pay the full overdue balance at once.
  - **Expected Output:** Offer a payment plan proposal with a structured repayment schedule and reduced interest rates or fees.
  - **Edge Case:** Cardholder accepts the proposal; ensure the plan is correctly applied to the account.

- **Test Case 5: Collection Agency Involvement**
  - **Input:** Cardholder fails to respond to previous notifications and reminders.
  - **Expected Output:** Card issuer involves a collection agency to recover the overdue balance.
  - **Edge Case:** Ensure the cardholder is notified of the collection agency involvement before any action is taken.

- **Test Case 6: Legal Action Initiation**
  - **Input:** Extreme cases of non-payment or default.
  - **Expected Output:** Card issuer initiates legal action against the cardholder.
  - **Edge Case:** Verify all prior steps and notifications have been completed before legal action is initiated.

**Non-Functional Test Cases**

- **Test Case 1: Performance of Notification System**
  - **Objective:** Ensure notifications are sent within a specific time frame (e.g., within 5 minutes of the trigger event).
  - **Expected Outcome:** Notifications are consistently sent within the expected time frame under normal and peak loads.

- **Test Case 2: Scalability of Notification System**
  - **Objective:** Assess the system's ability to handle a large number of notifications simultaneously.
  - **Expected Outcome:** System can handle increased load without degradation in performance.

- **Test Case 3: Security of Sensitive Information**
  - **Objective:** Verify that all notifications and communications do not expose sensitive cardholder information.
  - **Expected Outcome:** Notifications adhere to data protection regulations and do not include sensitive information like full card numbers.

- **Test Case 4: Usability of Notification Content**
  - **Objective:** Ensure notification content is clear and understandable to the average cardholder.
  - **Expected Outcome:** Cardholders can easily comprehend the notification message and take appropriate action.

- **Test Case 5: Reliability of Notification System**
  - **Objective:** Test the system's reliability over an extended period.
  - **Expected Outcome:** The system consistently performs its intended functions without failure over time.
